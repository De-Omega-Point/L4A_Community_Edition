Community Edition

Privacy Notice

Version 1.0

Loyalty-4-All Community Edition is designed to keep the business’s customer records on the device and browser where the product is used.

Business registration

To issue a Product ID, we ask for the business name, contact name and email address. Business type is optional. We use this information to issue and recover the Product ID, provide requested support and administer the Community Edition.

Optional contact

If the business selects the optional contact permission, we may send occasional feedback requests, one scheduled survey after approximately eight weeks, and useful product information. Permission can be withdrawn at any time.

Customer records

Customer names, contact details, visits, points, rewards, notes and QR codes are stored locally in the business’s browser. Community Edition does not automatically upload those records to De-Omega-Point.

Backups

The business controls its exported JSON and CSV files. The business is responsible for storing backups securely and managing access to the device.

Data minimisation

We do not sell Community Edition registration information or the business’s customer records. We collect only the registration information needed to issue and support the product.

Contact and corrections

Businesses may request access to, correction of or deletion of their registration information through the official De-Omega-Point contact channel published with the product.
