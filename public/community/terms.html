Free-forever licence

A registered business receives a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use Loyalty-4-All Community Edition for one business location. The Community Edition has no expiry and does not require a compulsory paid subscription.

Product ID

The Product ID identifies the registered Community Edition business. It is not a password and must not be used as proof of identity or authority over customer records.

Local records

Community Edition stores operational and customer records in the browser. The business is responsible for device security, authorised access, regular backups and compliance with laws applying to its collection and use of customer information.

Responsible use

The product must not be used for deceptive, unlawful, discriminatory or harmful conduct. Businesses must obtain appropriate permission before contacting customers.

Support and future services

Community Edition is self-service. Optional setup, support or future paid features may be offered separately. Declining a paid offer does not cancel the free-forever Community Edition licence.

Availability

We aim to maintain and improve the product but do not guarantee uninterrupted operation on every device or browser. Businesses should maintain current backups before installing updates or changing devices.
