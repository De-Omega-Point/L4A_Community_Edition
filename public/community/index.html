Use this browser as a fallback?

Installing the app is recommended, but installation alone does not protect your records. Both installed and browser workspaces need regular backups.

  • Clearing site data, private browsing, device loss or device problems can lose local records and registration access.
  • Changing browsers does not carry your records or registration receipt with you. There is no automatic migration or sync.
  • A customer backup restores records, not registration. A Product ID or registering again with the same email cannot recover access.

Have existing records? Download and check a backup before moving between browser and app. Use Download existing backup below; after access is confirmed, Settings & backup provides Download Backup and Restore. Keep backups outside this browser.

This choice is remembered only in this browser/profile on this site. We cannot reliably tell whether you are in private browsing.

Loyalty4All · Device removal

Back up before uninstalling

Your device handles removal. This button only opens safe instructions.

Keep your records before removing Loyalty4All. Close this guide and use Download Backup (or Download existing backup on the setup screen) and check that the file is saved somewhere safe outside this app. Export from each browser or installed app that holds records; they may be separate.

If a storage or backup warning appears, stop and keep your older backups. An export may contain only recovered or previously saved records. A backup does not restore your registration receipt.

Removing the app or clearing website data may permanently lose local customer records and registration access. Reinstalling does not automatically recover them.

This is guidance only. Loyalty4All cannot uninstall itself; nothing is removed when you open these instructions.

  • iPhone / iPad: Touch and hold the Loyalty4All icon. Choose Remove App, then Delete App and confirm if those options are shown. A saved shortcut may offer a different remove option. Removing only its Home Screen icon is not necessarily uninstalling the app.
  • Android: Open device Settings → Apps → See all apps → Loyalty4All → Uninstall. If it is only a shortcut, use your launcher’s remove option instead.
  • Chrome on a computer: Open the installed app, choose its top-right menu → Uninstall Loyalty4All → Remove. Leave “Also delete data from Chrome” unchecked if you want to keep browser data; do not rely on this instead of a backup.
  • Safari on Mac: In Finder, choose Go → Home → Applications, then move the Loyalty4All web app to the Bin. Other browsers may use different menus; follow their installed-app settings.

Device removal does not delete your server registration or withdraw marketing consent. For either request, contact Hello@De-Omega-Point.Com. No request is sent automatically.
