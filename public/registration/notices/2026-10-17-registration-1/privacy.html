Privacy Notice

Version 2026-10-17-registration-1

Registration is required before this version of the app can first be opened online. Your name and email are self-reported, not verified. Marketing consent is optional and is not a condition of app access.

Registration details stored online

We store whether you register as an individual or business, your contact name, business name when applicable, email address, a server-generated Product ID, registration time, and the versions and times of your terms and privacy acknowledgements. If you opt in to marketing, we also record that choice, its time and notice version. This information is kept in the app’s existing database, separately from your local customer records.

Remembering registration

A secure, HttpOnly browser cookie remembers that registration was completed. Only a one-way hash of that receipt is stored in the database; the public Product ID cannot unlock access. The cookie lasts up to one year, unless cleared sooner. A new browser, lost cookie or cleared browser data may require a new registration and create a new ID. This is not an account, identity check or recovery service.

Limited first-party observations

The app may record the first time this browser reports opening the app, requesting installation, and observing a supported browser’s installation-completed event. Registration is not proof of downloading or installation. Offline use, unsupported browsers and browser privacy settings may prevent observations. These records contain no customer information and use no third-party analytics, IP-address tracking or advertising identifiers in the registration system.

Your local business and customer records

Business settings, the existing local workspace ID, customer names, optional contact details, consent choices, visits, points, rewards, notes and QR codes stay in the browser on your device. They are not automatically uploaded to De-Omega-Point. The local workspace ID is separate from your registered Product ID and is not proof of identity.

Marketing and choices

Marketing is optional and unchecked by default. Registration alone does not permit marketing. No verification or marketing email is sent by this registration flow. Contact hello@de-omega-point.com to request a change to your registration details or withdraw marketing consent. Do not email customer records or a browser receipt. Requests need appropriate checks before private details are changed or disclosed.

Backups and device security

You control exported JSON and CSV backup files. Store them securely. Clearing browser data, losing a device or private browsing can remove local records; registration does not back up or sync them. Only a customer name is required for the local loyalty flow. Collect optional customer details only when needed and with appropriate permission.

Access, correction and deletion

You control local records through the customer screen and backups. Request access to, correction of or deletion of online registration details through hello@de-omega-point.com. Existing Community Intelligence records are retained separately; this update does not silently change their consent or convert them into verified accounts.
