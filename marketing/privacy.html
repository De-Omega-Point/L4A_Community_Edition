Privacy & data · 18 October 2026

Know where your information goes.

This notice covers the Loyalty-4-All product website and optional feedback and support messages. The Community Edition app has a separate privacy notice.

Browsing this website

This website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, account cookies or payment processors. Its hosting provider may process standard connection and security logs when you visit.

This product website does not collect registrations. The feedback form does not save your personal details to this site’s localStorage or send them to a registration server. Details stay in the page while you review them. Your browser may retain form or page state; use “Clear this message” when sharing a device.

Optional feedback and support

The form asks for your feedback or support question and a privacy acknowledgement. Your business name, preferred reply email and name are optional. Your email service shares your sending address when you send. It prepares a message to hello@de-omega-point.com. De-Omega-Point receives it only if you send it from your email service.

We use your message to understand feedback and provide requested support. Sending it does not register you for the app or subscribe you to updates. App registration happens separately on the app’s website before installation or use; marketing opt-in remains optional.

Email copies are held by your provider and the receiving email provider. Copying a message may place it on your clipboard; saving it creates a text file you control. Do not include passwords, payment details or your customers’ information.

Ask us by email to access, correct or delete message information. For earlier correspondence where you opted in to updates, you can also ask to stop them. We keep correspondence while needed to handle the request and any necessary records. Provider backups and obligations may affect deletion timing.

App registration and Product ID

Registration is required on the app’s website before installation or use. The app service saves registration contact details privately in its central database, separately from your local customer records. These include your name, email, registration type and business name when applicable, with registration and consent records. The details are supplied by you, not verified. Marketing opt-in is optional. Registration does not ask for a password.

The service issues a Product ID for the registration. This server-issued reference is not a password, verified identity or proof of paid access. The app’s local workspace ID is a separate on-device reference. A secure cookie on the app’s website remembers registration in that browser; a new browser or cleared cookie may require registration again. This is not an account or cross-device recovery service.

Community Edition customer records

The app stores customer, visit, points and reward records locally in the browser used for the app. It does not automatically upload those customer records to De-Omega-Point. App registration does not include local customer records.

Local storage is not a backup. Clearing browser data, switching browsers or losing a device may remove access to records. Export backups and protect the device and backup files. The app does not automatically synchronise records across devices.

Payments & old prototype data

No payment is taken here. This website does not issue paid entitlements, access serials or secure download links. Browser data and URL parameters cannot confirm payment.
