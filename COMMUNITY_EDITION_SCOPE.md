# Loyalty for All — Community Edition

## Promise

Community Edition is free forever. It has no expiry, requires no credit card and does not remotely disable itself when a business declines surveys, marketing or paid services.

## Core loop

**Scan → Revisit → Reward → Backup**

## Included

- One registered business location
- First-run business registration and permanent Product ID
- Local customer records
- Customer QR codes and scannable loyalty cards
- Camera and manual code lookup
- Visits, points and one configurable basic reward
- Lightweight dashboard and customer history
- Optional contact-consent record
- PNG and A4 card output
- JSON backup/restore and customer CSV export
- Safe, explicit demo mode
- Installable offline-capable PWA
- Quiet, optional feedback pathway

## Explicit non-goals

- No business login or customer login
- No cloud customer database or automatic customer-data upload
- No multi-user staff permissions
- No multi-location synchronisation
- No automated SMS or email campaigns
- No POS, payments, Stripe, PayPal, Square or Xero integration
- No AI or predictive recommendations
- No advanced analytics or benchmarking
- No referrals, birthday automation or customer portal
- No compulsory subscription

## Product registration boundary

The central registration service may store only the registered business information needed to issue and support the Product ID. Customer loyalty records remain local by default.

## Development gate

A future paid capability should not enter development until at least three active businesses request it and demonstrate willingness to pay.
